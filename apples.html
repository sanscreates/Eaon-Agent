🍎 Apples

Nature's crunchiest snack

What is an apple?

The apple (Malus domestica) is a deciduous tree fruit from Central Asia. Cultivated for thousands of years, it's the most widely grown tree fruit — over 7,500 varieties exist worldwide. Apples are pomaceous fruit: the fleshy part develops from the flower's receptacle, not the ovary.

Popular varieties

🟢

Granny Smith

Bright green, tart, firm. Great for pies. Originated in Australia, 1868.

🔴

Red Delicious

Deep red, sweet, mild. The classic lunchbox apple. Most grown in US for decades.

🟡

Golden Delicious

Yellow-green, sweet, tender. Good raw or in salads. Chance seedling from West Virginia.

🟠

Honeycrisp

Red-yellow, explosively crisp, honey-sweet. Developed at University of Minnesota, 1991.

🔴

Fuji

Pink-red, very sweet, dense. Developed in Japan, cross of Red Delicious × Ralls Janet.

🟢

Pink Lady

Pink blush, tart-sweet, crisp. Late-season. Developed in Western Australia.

Fun facts

  • Apple trees take 4–5 years to produce first fruit.
  • Apple seeds contain amygdalin — can release cyanide if crushed. You'd need ~150 seeds to get sick.
  • The saying "an apple a day keeps the doctor away" dates to 1860s Wales.
  • China grows nearly half the world's apples.
  • There are over 2,500 varieties grown in the US alone.
  • Apples float because 25% of their volume is air.
  • The world's largest apple weighed 4 pounds 1 ounce (2018, Japan).

Apple idioms

  • Bad apple — a corrupting influence
  • Apples and oranges — incomparable things
  • How do you like them apples? — triumphant rhetorical question
  • Upset the apple cart — spoil a plan
